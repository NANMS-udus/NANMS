Advancing Healthcare Leadership Across Niger State

Key session tracks scheduled for the plenary convention include:

  • Clinical Skills Workshops: Hands-on trauma resuscitation, FAST ultrasound simulation, and bedside procedural OSCE masterclasses.
  • Poster Presentations & Scientific Review: Peer-reviewed epidemiology papers and community public health survey dissemination.
  • Keynote Plenary Address: Delivered by the State Commissioner for Health and senior academic consultants.

All registered delegates are required to confirm chapter delegate badges at the secretariat accreditation desk prior to the keynote address.
