New Clinical Rotations Schedule Announced for 2025 Academic Year

Image: Medical students reviewing the new scheduling framework in the primary campus library.

The National Association of Niger State Medical Students, in collaboration with the university administration, is pleased to announce the finalized clinical rotation schedule for the upcoming 2025 academic year.

Key Changes to the Framework

This comprehensive update aims to optimize student exposure across critical specialties while ensuring compliance with national accreditation standards. The new schedule reflects extensive feedback from both faculty and the student body.

  • Extended primary care modules in rural districts to enhance community medicine competence.
  • Restructured surgical rotations allowing for focused, continuous block scheduling.
  • Introduction of simulated emergency response drills within the core curriculum.

Implementation Timeline

The transition to the new schedule will commence immediately following the conclusion of the current semester. Students currently enrolled in 4th-year programs will receive their individualized placement details via the university portal by November 15th.

Action Required

All clinical students must verify their current registration status in the portal before the scheduling algorithm finalizes placements on November 1st.
