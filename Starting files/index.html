Our Legacy & Vision

Founded in the heart of Niger State, built on a foundation of rigorous academic pursuit and unwavering commitment to public health.

Fostering Academic Excellence

NANMS was established to provide a supportive network for medical students originating from or studying in Niger State. We bridge the gap between rigorous medical training and professional development.

Our mission extends beyond the classroom. We envision a healthier future driven by competent, compassionate scholar-physicians who are deeply connected to their communities.
